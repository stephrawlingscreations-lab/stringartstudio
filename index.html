How to Use the String Art Designer

1. Choose your board
Select a circle or square and choose how many nails your board will have.

2. Create your pattern
Click one nail, then another to draw a thread line between them.

3. Continue connecting nails
Build up your design by connecting nails in sequence.

4. Follow the thread sequence
Use the sequence panel to follow the order when wrapping your thread.

Tip: Use Undo if you make a mistake.
