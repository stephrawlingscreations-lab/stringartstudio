Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to create an account to use the designer? +
No account, no signup, no download — nothing. Open the designer and start designing immediately. Your work is saved automatically in your browser so it's there when you come back.
What board shapes does it support? +
The designer supports circle, square, diamond, hexagon, and heart boards. The nail placement guide also includes a star shape for printable templates.
How does the nail sequence work? +
Each nail on your board is numbered. A sequence is simply the order you visit them — for example, "1, 8, 15, 22…" tells you exactly which nail to loop your thread around next. The designer generates this for you based on your pattern, and you can print it out to follow step by step at your board.
Can I use this for a board I've already made? +
Yes. Set the nail count in the designer to match your board, choose the right shape, and design your pattern digitally. You'll get a sequence to follow on the physical board you already have.
What's the nail placement guide for? +
It generates a printable template at your exact board size. Print it at 100% scale (no "fit to page"), tape it to your board, and use it to mark nail positions perfectly evenly — no measuring, no guessing.
Is it really free? +
The core designer and nail placement guide are completely free. There's an optional Pro upgrade that unlocks multi-layer designs for more complex patterns, but everything you need to plan and build a great piece is free.
