The maker behind the tools

I made the tools I couldn't find anywhere else.

I'm Steph — I came to string art as a complete beginner, excited to get started but quickly frustrated when I couldn't find any decent tools to help me plan my designs.

Every app I found was either too complicated, not built for string art, or just didn't do what I actually needed. So I decided to build my own.

These tools are made by someone who's been exactly where you are — starting from scratch, figuring it out as I went, and wishing something like this already existed. I hope they make your projects a little easier and a lot more satisfying.
