Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Will this actually work in real life? +
Yes — the entire tool is built to be used on a real board. You get exact nail positions, a full step-by-step sequence, and printable guides. If you follow the sequence, your design will come out exactly as shown.
Do I need experience to use this? +
No — beginners can use this. The sequence tells you exactly where to start, where to go next, and when to stop. No guessing, no figuring it out yourself.
What do I actually get with Pro? +
You get a full build guide: a printable nail placement template, complete numbered thread sequence, colour breakdown, thread estimate, and a clean layout you can follow while building. It's everything you need to go from screen to finished piece.
Will it print correctly? +
Yes — the templates are designed for true-size printing. Just print at 100% (no scaling) and follow the guide. Everything lines up exactly on your board.
What if I just use the free version? +
The free version lets you design and test ideas. But Pro is what makes it usable in real life — it turns your design into a proper step-by-step build guide.
Is this a one-time payment? +
Yes — it's a one-time purchase per design. No subscriptions, no hidden costs.
Will I lose my design if I close the tab? +
No — use the Export file button in the designer to save your design as a .sas file to your device. You can reload it any time using Load from file, even weeks later or on a different device. There's also a quick Save button that stores your current design in the browser for within-session recovery.
What size boards can I use? +
Any size. You set the board dimensions in centimetres — the designer and the nail placement guide both scale to match exactly. Whether you're working on a small 20 cm circle or a large 60 cm square, everything adjusts: nail positions, the printable template, and the Pro build pack all output at true scale for your board.
