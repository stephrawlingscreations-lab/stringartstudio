How to Use the String Art Designer

1. Choose your board settings
Pick your shape, number of nails, and any other settings you want.

2. On phones and tablets
Scroll down and tap Open Draw Mode to open the full-screen drawing board. This is the easiest way to use the tool on a mobile.

3. Start your pattern
Tap one nail, then tap another to draw a line between them.

4. Keep building your design
Continue tapping nails in order to create your pattern.

5. Check your sequence
The sequence shows the order of the nails you selected.

Tip: Use Undo if you make a mistake.
