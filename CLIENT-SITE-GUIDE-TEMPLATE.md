## 5) Backing Up Your Data
Do this regularly (recommended: monthly).

1. Log into your app
2. Go to `[Reports / Settings / Backup area]`
3. Click **Export backup** (or **Backup**)
4. Save the file in:
   - OneDrive / Google Drive
   - Your computer
   - Optional: email a copy to yourself

### Restore steps
1. Log into your app
2. Go to backup/restore section
3. Click **Import backup** / **Restore**
4. Select your saved file
